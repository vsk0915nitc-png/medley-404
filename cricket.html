HOW THE BRACKET WORKS

  1. A total of 24 teams will participate in the tournament.
  2. In the Round of 24, all 24 teams will play, resulting in 12 winners.
  3. The 12 winning teams will qualify directly for the Round of 16.
  4. The remaining 4 qualification spots will be awarded to the best-performing teams based on Net Run Rate (NRR), making a total of 16 qualified teams.
  5. The Round of 16 will consist of 16 teams playing 8 knockout matches, with the 8 winners advancing to the Quarterfinals.
  6. The Quarterfinals will consist of 8 teams playing 4 matches, with the 4 winners advancing to the Semifinals.
  7. The Semifinals will consist of 4 teams playing 2 matches, with the 2 winners advancing to the Final.
  8. The winners of the two Semifinals will play in the Final to determine the Champion.
